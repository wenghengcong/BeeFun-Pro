# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on technical issues, not personal attacks.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination
- Trolling or inflammatory comments
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
